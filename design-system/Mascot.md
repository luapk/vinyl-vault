# Mascot

The trunk is an aluminium record flight case that walks. A scowling face sits on its front panel under the latch; it has thin black rubber-hose arms and legs, white four-finger gloves and white shoes. It comes in two treatments.

## Flat ink

Thick black outlines, flat white panels, halftone dot shading on the shadowed side, and nothing else: no grey fills, no gradients. 1930s rubber-hose cartoon. This is the treatment the product uses: the splash clips, the four walkthrough clips and the app icon.

- **It sits on `acid` and only on acid.** The clips ship with a transparent background and take the acid from the card they sit on (`#cafe04`, the walkthrough card). Never bake a background into a clip: each of the first four baked a slightly different green, and every one showed as a rectangle on the card.
- Display height in the walkthrough is 168px, so one clear action per clip, legible at that size.

## 3D render

Brushed and scuffed aluminium with rivets and corner caps, soft white gloves and shoes, matte black limbs. For marketing, social and campaign art, where it can stand in a scene (the desert walk) or on acid. The rigged model `GrumpyTrunk_rigged.glb` has one animation, `GrumpyStomp_RigTest` (about 2s, a stomping walk); the `3d-*` stills here are rendered from it on a transparent ground.

## Character rules

- **Deadpan, never cheerful.** The face is faintly scowling and stays that way. No grinning, no thumbs up, no cheering, no bouncing. That is the difference between a collector and a mascot. `flat-open-grin.webp` breaks this rule and is kept as a reference for what to avoid.
- **It acts, it does not perform.** One purposeful action: shoots a photo, studies a label through a loupe, flicks through the crate inside its own lid, walks off to play out. It may hold a prop (camera, magnifying glass, headphones).
- **The lid opening shows records.** When the lid is up, there are sleeves and a record inside. That is the whole product in one picture.
- **Never in place of the logo, and never wearing it.** No text in mascot art. The character never sits inside the lozenge or beside the script lockup as a pair.
- **Same line weight, same scale** across a set, so it does not appear to change size as someone pages through.
- To generate new art, reference a frame of `clip-splash.webp` (the full-body character on acid), not `intro.mp4`, which is the faceless logo animation.
