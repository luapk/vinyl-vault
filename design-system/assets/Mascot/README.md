The trunk mascot. `clip-*` are the shipped animated WebPs (780 x 440, transparent, 16fps): `clip-step-*` the four walkthrough steps (scan, confirm, file, play), `clip-splash*` the splash rotation with `clip-splash-flight` as the hero. Place them on `acid`. `flat-*` are flat ink stills on acid; `flat-open-grin` is off character (it grins) and is kept as a what-not-to-do. `render-walk-desert` and `3d-*` are the 3D treatment; `3d-*` are rendered from GrumpyTrunk_rigged.glb (front, three-quarter, side, back, and three frames of the stomp) on a transparent ground.
