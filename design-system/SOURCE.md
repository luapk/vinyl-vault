# Design system source

The source files of the Found Sound design system, published as a Design System artifact at https://claude.ai/artifact/TRVeS1nshZoHa1Hqzf656C (private until shared).

Extracted from `src/index.css`, `tailwind.config.js`, `src/components/*.jsx`, `src/lib/brand.js`, `brand/README.md` and `public/walkthrough/BRIEFS.md`. The artifact is the live copy: edits made on its page do not flow back here.

Only text and the two script logos live here. Every other image in the artifact is already in `public/`, apart from the uploaded mascot art and the `3d-*` stills rendered from `GrumpyTrunk_rigged.glb`, which are stored in the artifact only.

The script logos in `assets/Logos/` are hand-supplied artwork, not output of `brand/stamp.py` or `brand/stacked.py`. Do not copy them into `public/` by hand; route them through `brand/` if they ship.
