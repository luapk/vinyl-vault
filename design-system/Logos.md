# Logos

Every lockup is outlines (the stamp's subline included), so the logo never waits on a webfont and carries no font licence. Each comes as separate colour files; pick the file for the ground, never recolour one with CSS.

| Lockup | Files | Aspect | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Block | `logo-block-acid/white/black.svg` | 1.33:1 | The app header, at 48px tall. |
| Stamp | `logo-stamp-acid/white/black.svg` | 2.77:1 | Footer, sign-in, pricing. Minimum 48px tall on 2x screens, 56px on 1x, or the subline turns to mush. |
| Mark | `logo-mark-acid/white/black.svg` | 3.90:1 | The stamp without the subline, for anywhere too small to read it: the static legal pages. |
| Script | `logo-script-black/white.svg` | 1.36:1 | New. Display and marketing: the splash, social, merch, print, campaign email headers. |

## Colourways

- `acid` (`#cafe04`) on `bg` and on `ink`.
- `white` (`logo-white`, `#f7f8f2`) on `bg`, on `ink` and on photography.
- `black` (`logo-black`, `#0d0c0a`) on `acid` and on the light page.
- Never acid on the light page, and never any logo on a crate colour.

## Rules

- **Block in the header, nothing beside it.** It replaced the horizontal mark because the mark took 132.5px of a 360px header and made the nav row scroll; the block takes 64px. Header height is 48px.
- **The stamp's subline is part of the drawing.** Do not set "YOUR VINYL, SORTED" as live text under a mark to imitate it; use the stamp, or use the tagline in the eyebrow slot in its own mono voice.
- **Do not hand-edit block, stamp or mark files.** In the repo they are generated by `brand/stamp.py` and `brand/stacked.py` from the potrace traces; the next run overwrites any edit.
- **Script is a fourth lockup, not a replacement.** It is the brand at its loudest, so it is used large and alone. Do not use it in the app header, at under 120px wide, inside the lozenge, or next to another lockup. It carries its own offset keyline, so give it clear space of at least one letter height on every side and do not add a second outline.
- The script files are not yet produced by the brand scripts and are not in `public/`. If they ship in the app, add them through the toolchain in `brand/`, not by hand.
- Give every logo `alt="Found Sound"` (the script SVGs already carry `aria-label="Found Sound"`).
