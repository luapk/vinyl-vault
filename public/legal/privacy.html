What we collect

Because you gave it to us

  • Your email address and password, held by our authentication provider. We never see your password.
  • Your display name, and if you choose to set them, a username, a short bio and an avatar image.
  • Your records: artist, title, label, catalogue number, year, country, format, genres, your own tags, your crate names, notes and tracklists.
  • Photographs you take of sleeves and labels.
  • Your wishlist, and the results of any price research you run on it.
  • Messages and comments you post to other users.

Because you paid us

  • Your subscription tier, its status, when it renews and what it cost.
  • Identifiers linking you to your Stripe customer and subscription records. We never receive or store your card details.

Because the service generated it

  • How many AI scans you have used in the current period, to apply the free-plan limit.
  • A record of each AI identification request, its token counts and its cost, so we can see what the service costs to run.
  • Ordinary server logs, which include IP addresses, kept by our hosting provider.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising trackers.

What is stored on your device

Found Sound keeps a copy of your collection in your browser's local storage so the app works offline and opens instantly. It is tied to your account and stays on that device until you sign out or clear your browser data. We do not use cookies for advertising or analytics.

What other people can see

Your profile is private by default. If you make it public, your username, display name, bio, avatar, record count and collection become visible to other signed-in users, and your profile can be shared by link. Your email address is never shown to other users. You can make your profile private again at any time.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and collection: until you delete your account, then removed within 30 days.
  • Payment records: six years after the transaction, which UK tax law requires.
  • Server and error logs: typically 30 to 90 days, depending on the provider.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or send it to another service. You can withdraw consent to marketing at any time, using the unsubscribe link in any email or the setting in your account.

Email [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] and we will respond within one month. If you are not happy with our answer you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Found Sound is not for under-16s and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
