Cancellation and Refunds

Last updated 4 November 2026
Before launch: fill in the support email, and make sure the checkout actually asks for the acknowledgement described in section 1. Without it you cannot rely on the exception, and a customer keeps the full 14-day right even after using the service.

1. Your 14-day right to cancel

Because you buy Found Sound online, UK law gives you 14 days from the date of purchase to change your mind and get your money back, without giving a reason.

There is one important exception, and it applies here. If you ask us to start the service immediately, and you acknowledge at checkout that you will lose the right to cancel once it begins, then that right ends when your access starts. Our checkout asks for exactly that, because the point of buying is to use it straight away.

Even so, we will refund you in full within 14 days of purchase if you have barely used the service. We would rather give the money back than hold someone to a plan they do not want. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL].

2. Cancelling a subscription

Cancel whenever you like, from Account, then Manage subscription. It takes one click and no email to us.

When you cancel:

  • You keep full access until the end of the period you have already paid for.
  • Nothing is taken from you again.
  • Your records stay where they are. Your account drops to the free Digger plan at the end of the period. Nothing is deleted.

We do not refund the remainder of a year you are part way through, except in the cases in section 3.

3. When we refund anyway

  • A renewal you did not expect. If you were charged for a new year and had not meant to continue, tell us within 14 days of the charge and we will refund it in full and cancel.
  • The service was broken for you. If a feature central to your plan did not work and we could not fix it, we will refund the unused part of the period.
  • We withdrew something. If we remove a feature that was a material part of your plan, you can cancel and we will refund the unused part.
  • We closed your account for a reason other than your breach of the terms.

4. The founding offer

The £59 founding purchase is a single payment, not a subscription. It does not renew and there is nothing to cancel. The 14-day right in section 1 applies to it in the same way.

5. How to reach us

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. Tell us the address on the account and roughly when you bought. We aim to answer within two working days.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe. Your bank normally shows it within five to ten working days.

6. This does not replace your legal rights

Nothing here reduces your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 or the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. If something we sold you was not as described, not fit for purpose, or not supplied with reasonable care and skill, you have remedies in law regardless of what this page says.
